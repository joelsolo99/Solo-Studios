    <p>Hi, I’m <strong>Joel Solomons</strong>, and I run <strong>Solo Studios</strong>. I’m a Royal College of Music-trained saxophonist and clarinettist, and I’ve spent years bringing live music to weddings, parties, and events across the South West. Based in Plymouth, I love creating unforgettable moments with music that gets people dancing, singing, and celebrating.</p>
    
    <p>At Solo Studios, we offer a range of services: live saxophone performances, full DJ sets, or the perfect combination of both with our high-energy Sax & DJ experience. Whether it’s an intimate wedding, a milestone birthday, a corporate function, or an all-out party, we bring the right atmosphere every time. Our repertoire is massive, covering everything from smooth jazz to Ibiza anthems, ensuring the music matches your vision.</p>
    
    <p>We don’t just bring the music – we bring the full show. With a fully programmable lighting rig, a professional PA system, wireless mics, and top-tier equipment, we make sure your event looks and sounds amazing. Whether you want chilled background vibes or a packed dancefloor until the early hours, we’ve got it covered.</p>
    
    <p>We’re all about personalising the experience for you. If you’re planning an event and want to chat about how we can make it truly special, <a href="mailto:events@solostudios.uk">get in touch</a>. Let’s make some incredible memories together!</p>

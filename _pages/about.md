    <p>Hi, I’m <strong>Joel Solomons</strong>, and I run <strong>Solo Studios</strong>. I’m a Royal College of Music-trained saxophonist and clarinettist, and I’ve spent years bringing live music to weddings, parties, and events across the South West. Based in Plymouth, I love getting people up on the dancefloor, singing along and dancing to some of my favourite music.</p>
    
    <p>At Solo Studios, we offer a range of sets: live sax performances, full DJ sets, or a combination of both with our Sax & DJ experience. Whether it’s a small wedding, a big birthday, a work night out, or all in on party anthems, we bring the right atmosphere every time. Our repertoire is massive, covering smooth jazz to Ibiza anthems, rock and roll to reggae, and everything in between, ensuring we can always match your vision. </p>
    
    <p>We don’t just bring the music – we bring the full show. With a fully programmable lighting rig, a professional PA system (with optional additional thumping bass units), wireless mics, and modern equipment, we make sure your event both looks and sounds amazing. If you want some quiet cocktail jazz in the background or a packed dancefloor until the small hours, we’ve got you.</p>

    <p>Our DJ has over <strong>five years of experience</strong> behind the decks and was previously a house DJ at a local venue. He’s performed at weddings, private parties, and corporate events, always matching the music to the energy of the room.</p>

    <p>His music knowledge is very impressive, and spans a huge range of genres, from <strong>modern chart-toppers</strong> to <strong>classic pop sing-alongs</strong>, and he knows how to read the crowd to keep the dancefloor full. He’s also well-versed in music from many global cultures, ensuring your event soundtrack is tailored to you. Need someone to make an announcement? He’s happy to be on the mic – within reason!</p>

    <p>We are very flexible and would like to plan our performances around you: if there's a song you can't go without, we probably already have it - and if not we are always happy to learn new music! If you’re planning an event and want to discuss how we can make it truly special, <a href="mailto:events@solostudios.uk">get in touch</a>. We'd love to have a chat!</p>
